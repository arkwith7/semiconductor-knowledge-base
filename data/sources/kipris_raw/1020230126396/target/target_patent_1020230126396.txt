Application No.(Date) Related Application No. Request for an examination(Date) Y(2023.09.21) Number of examination claims 1 KPA Abstract Disclosed is a through silicon via etching method for forming a through silicon via on a semiconductor wafer made of a silicon material. According to the present invention, a method for etching a through silicon via comprises the steps of: preparing an acute semiconductor wafer of which a bottom surface and a side surface of an edge form an acute angle; forming an oxide film by performing an oxidation process on the entire surface of the acute semiconductor wafer; forming a nitride film in contact with the oxide film on the entire surface of the acute semiconductor wafer on which the oxide film is formed to protect the edge of the acute semiconductor wafer; forming an etching mask pattern by patterning the nitride film and the oxide film; and forming a through via hole in the acute semiconductor wafer by using the etching mask pattern.
